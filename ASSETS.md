## Dice artwork · 2.1.1

src/assets/dice/die-1.svg through die-6.svg are original native vector
drawings. Warm ivory faces, metallic bevels and recessed indigo pips are
defined in src/dice-core.mjs and exported by scripts/build-dice.mjs.
There is no printed numeral on any face. Inline copies avoid external image
requests during a roll; unique gradient identifiers keep multiple dice
and history entries independent. The artwork is not a generated bitmap.

## Spanish and American playing cards

src/assets/playing-cards/ contains 100 original faces: 48 Spanish and 52
American. Twenty-four court illustrations were generated with the built-in
image-generation tool, one request per card; full briefs and saved paths are
in src/playing-cards-art.json. The images are encoded as WebP at their original
resolution. Seventy-six pip compositions are precise native SVG engravings
generated by scripts/build-playing-cards.py. The map is src/playing-cards.json.
Spanish cards keep Sota, Caballo and Rey; American courts are double-headed.
These designs are contemporary originals inspired by the respective traditions.

## Candle and crystal rooms

src/contemplation.mjs paints wax, turned brass, a layered flame, refractive
glass, moving indigo veils and amber reflections directly in Canvas 2D.
No downloaded or generated bitmap is used for either animation. Measured flow
and aggregate journal notices shape the painting; slow brush motion remains
an explicit artistic component. Demo signals are separately labeled.

## Tarot de Marseille

The original Kamala deck contains 78 portrait images under src/assets/tarot/.
The 22 major arcana and 16 court cards were created individually with the
built-in image generation tool, using Le Bateleur as a visual reference for
ivory paper, French woodblock linework and flat red, indigo, gold and green.
The site uses WebP copies at the generated resolution. The 40 numeral cards
are code-native SVG designs rebuilt by scripts/build-tarot.py, with exact
counts of the corresponding suit emblems and floral borders.

src/tarot.json is the full card-to-image map and prompt set. This is an
original Marseille-inspired deck, not a facsimile of a historic printer's deck.
The public gallery is /apps/cards/tarot/. Live HTML provides names and ranks;
the image files contain no generated lettering. Justice is VIII, Strength XI,
and Le Mat remains unnumbered. Cards can be enlarged from either spread layout.

## Bronze coins

| Side | Source asset | Output asset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heads | src/assets/coin-relief-v2.png | dist/assets/coin-relief-v2.png |
| Tails | src/assets/coin-cross-relief-v2.png | dist/assets/coin-cross-relief-v2.png |

Both are 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG assets with transparent corners. The tails
asset was created with the built-in image generation tool using the existing
portrait coin as the material, rim, lighting and framing reference. Neither
asset depends on a generated-image download URL at runtime.

The shared coin renderer uses both assets at the same displayed size. It
also supplies the three-coin I Ching casts and the technique-page experiments.
Uploaded local portraits keep their existing photo treatment; the bronze
reverse is retained.

## Reverse-side brief

Create the reverse side of the reference bronze portrait coin: one large,
perfectly centered, traditional eight-pointed Maltese cross in deep sculptural
bas relief. Four equal wedge-shaped arms expand from a narrow center along
the cardinal directions. Each outer arm has a deep V notch, giving exactly
eight crisp tips. Match the reference's warm antique bronze-gold metal,
softly worn texture, circular diameter, straight-on view, double raised rim
and beaded inner border. Raise the cross above a recessed bronze field, with
thick beveled edges, upper-left highlights and lower-right relief shadows.
Keep the cross clear of the border. Use a transparent background outside
the circular coin. No portrait, text, date, numerals, ribbons, other symbols
or watermark.
