## Classical Chinese text

The displayed Chinese passages are the 64 hexagram statements (卦辭) of the
received Zhouyi (周易), also known in English as the Book of Changes. They are
distinguished from the individual line statements and later commentaries.
“Original” in the data means the classical Chinese passage, not a claim to
reproduce an ancient manuscript or an author's autograph.

The digital text witness is **Wikisource, Chinese-language edition**, contributed
by Wikisource editors:

https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/周易

Each reading includes a direct link to its own hexagram page. SOURCES.json
records all 64 source URLs, raw-text URLs, retrieval timestamps and SHA-256
hashes of the retrieved raw witnesses. Sources were retrieved on 2026-11-07.

Chinese characters and variant forms in this witness are retained, including
无. Wikitext markup, bold markers and inline variant annotations are omitted.
Display whitespace is normalized. In hexagram 23, the digital witness's
punctuation “剝：不利。有攸往。” is displayed as “剝：不利有攸往。”: the Chinese
wording is unchanged and the sentence is not split after 不利. This punctuation
change is also recorded in SOURCES.json.

## Brief English renderings

The English passages are **brief editorial renderings prepared for Kamala**.
They summarize each classical hexagram statement and are labeled accordingly
in the interface. They are not presented as an official translation, a complete
scholarly edition, or words supplied by Wikisource or the Chinese Text Project.
Interpretive choices such as rendering 貞 as steadfastness or constancy are
editorial choices. Traditional historical wording remains visible in Chinese.
