HISTORICAL METHOD

Steiner and Pfeiffer

Steiner's 1924 Agriculture Course provides the historical context. Pfeiffer's sensitive crystallization work documents an image-forming practice with copper salts. Later experiments make preparation effects such as wetting part of the record.

CONTEMPLATIVE HYPOTHESIS

The observer's footprint

The proposal is that the observer changes polarization at emission and that the light influences crystal growth. The orientation record describes the visible crystal axes. Testing the proposal calls for independent measurements of the incident light and blinded comparisons of separately prepared samples.

THE OBSERVATION

Look closely.

Follow the axes that appear as a liquid crystallizes. The north-aligned microscope provides a reference for studying visible growth alongside the image-forming tradition associated with Rudolf Steiner and Ehrenfried Pfeiffer.
