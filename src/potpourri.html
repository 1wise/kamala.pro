BEFORE THE PHOTOGRAPH

Keep the detail.
Keep the context.

  1. Use soft, even light and a steady camera. Check focus before sending.
  2. Include one full view and one closer detail. Preserve the order of a spread or sequence.
  3. Describe the question, procedure and what happened before the photograph.
  4. Include only material that can be shared. Keep addresses and unrelated people outside the frame.

How photographs are handled

Images are checked and re-encoded before being attached to an email. Temporary processed images are deleted after the sending attempt; there is no permanent upload directory. A copy remains in the inquiry email for the interpretation and follow-up. Deletion can be requested by replying to that correspondence.
