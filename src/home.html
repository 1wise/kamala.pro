OUR APPROACH / 03

Curiosity without
a prescribed belief.

Observe carefully. Record honestly.
Leave room for doubt.

Kamala brings natural observation and cultural practices into the same conversation, while keeping their methods distinct. A measurement, a symbolic reading and a personal experience are different kinds of evidence.

Christian, Sikh, yogic, shamanic and other traditions can be studied in their own contexts, without assuming that their practices or meanings are interchangeable.

Oral transmission. Public vocabulary.

Kamala follows an oral-transmission commitment in its Roma and Karelian references: reserved mantic teachings are not transcribed. The public site offers vocabulary, tools and technical observations; it does not reproduce those teachings or ritual instructions. This is the project’s editorial commitment, not a claim that every community follows one rule.
