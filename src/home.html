OUR APPROACH / 03

Curiosity without
a prescribed belief.

Observe carefully. Record honestly.
Leave room for doubt.

Kamala brings natural observation and cultural practices into the same conversation, while keeping their methods distinct. A measurement, a symbolic reading and a personal experience are different kinds of evidence.

Christian, Sikh, yogic, shamanic and other traditions can be studied in their own contexts, without assuming that their practices or meanings are interchangeable.
