WINDCHIME / OPTICAL WIND INDICATOR

Ladle and wok.
Motion in relation.

Waiting for measurements

Two moving references.

The reflective ladle handle is the first tracked reference. The second belongs to the wok body. Their relative motion, rather than either object's movement alone, is the subject of the windchime observation.

ReferenceWhat the current tracker follows
Reflective ladle handleThe bright handle's horizontal position and its changes over time.
WokThe cavity center as a separate anchor, with orientation estimated along the wok's handle. This reference belongs to the wok, not to the ladle.

DH combines variation in the handle-to-wok offset with a loss of coherence between handle motion and wok tilt. It is an experimental optical indicator used by the source's alarm logic.

Observation hypothesis: increasing divergence may accompany irregular airflow. A possible association with low atmospheric pressure must be compared with an independent barometer record; the video and the 13-field stream do not measure pressure in hPa. READING THE INSTRUMENT

Image, motion,
observation.

Position & reference
The detected bell center, apparent radius and slowly adapting equilibrium reference remain in image pixels.
Offset & velocity
X / Y / Z movement is transmitted in millimeters and millimeters per second. Positive Z points away from the lens. The overhead diagram plots physical X against depth Z. Y remains in the readout as the vertical component.
Fit & calibration
Geometric fit is a matching score, not a probability or brightness reading. Samples at 15% or below are omitted from the traces. Z is inferred from apparent size; its accuracy requires a physical calibration test.
