## Remote viewing

A target-based practice in which a person records impressions of a concealed or distant scene for later comparison.

The term became prominent in twentieth-century parapsychology and government-funded research. Published protocols and their assessments disagree about interpretation; an impression remains a report until compared under a defined procedure.

- Remote viewing / Visión remota: Recording impressions of a target withheld from the participant, then comparing the complete record with the target.

- CIA — Remote-viewing program and evaluation history: https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-did-cia-really-study-psychic-powers
- SRI archival report — Blind differential judging procedures: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00787R000100060001-6.pdf

## Precognition & presentiment

Accounts interpreted as anticipating a later event, through a specific impression or a more general feeling.

Parapsychological usage distinguishes a described future event from an anticipatory feeling. Dated records and predetermined comparisons help separate the original statement from later reinterpretation.

- Precognition / Precognición: A reported impression or image interpreted as information about an event that has not yet occurred.
- Presentiment / Presentimiento: An anticipatory feeling interpreted as relating to a later event, often without a specific scene or prediction.

- APA Dictionary — Precognition: https://dictionary.apa.org/precognition
- APA Dictionary — Extrasensory perception: https://dictionary.apa.org/extrasensory-perception

## Retrocognition

Impressions interpreted as referring to past events that the person reports not knowing beforehand.

The term belongs to parapsychological vocabulary. Prior knowledge, suggestive descriptions and access to historical information affect how a later comparison can be interpreted.

- Retrocognition / Retrocognición: A reported experience interpreted as knowledge of an earlier event through means beyond ordinary recollection or inference.
- Postcognition / Postcognición: An alternative term for retrocognition.

- APA Dictionary — Retrocognition: https://dictionary.apa.org/retrocognition

## Telepathy

Reports interpreted as information passing between people without an identified ordinary sensory channel.

Telepathy is a term in parapsychology rather than a demonstrated communication service. Records are more useful when participants cannot exchange visual, verbal or timing clues during a trial.

- Telepathy / Telepatía: A claimed transfer of thoughts or impressions between minds without an ordinary sensory explanation.

- APA Dictionary — Extrasensory perception: https://dictionary.apa.org/extrasensory-perception

## Psychometry

A practice in which impressions about an object or its associations are recorded while holding or observing it.

Here psychometry has its parapsychological meaning, distinct from psychometrics and psychological measurement. Nineteenth-century accounts are historical testimony to the practice and its claims.

- Psychometry / Psicometría de objetos: Reported impressions about an object’s history or associations, attributed by practitioners to contact with the object.
- Psychometrics / Psicometría psicológica: The measurement of psychological characteristics; a separate meaning of a similar word.

- APA Dictionary — Psychometry: https://dictionary.apa.org/psychometry
- Wellcome Collection — Denton, The Soul of Things (1871): https://wellcomecollection.org/works/ca3b8mtv

## Clairaudience & clairsentience

Terms used for experiences interpreted as intuitive hearing or feeling, often described as internal impressions.

These words have varying meanings in spiritual and parapsychological writing. This entry documents the reported sensory quality and the interpretation offered by its source, without treating the experience as an independently verified message.

- Clairaudience / Clariaudiencia: An experience interpreted as hearing information beyond an identified ordinary auditory source.
- Clairsentience / Clarisentiencia: A feeling or bodily impression interpreted as intuitive information; usage varies by practitioner.

- APA Dictionary — Clairaudience: https://dictionary.apa.org/clairaudience
- APA Dictionary — Extrasensory perception: https://dictionary.apa.org/extrasensory-perception

## Oneiromancy

The interpretation of dreams as symbolic signs or omens within a named cultural or personal framework.

Dream interpretation has distinct literary, religious and psychological histories. Artemidorus is one surviving ancient author; his correspondences describe his setting rather than a universal dictionary for every dreamer.

- Oneiromancy / Oniromancia: Divinatory or symbolic interpretation of dreams.
- Dream interpretation / Interpretación de sueños: Assigning or exploring meanings in a dream through a stated framework.

- Wellcome Collection — Artemidorus, The Interpretation of Dreams: https://wellcomecollection.org/works/jwyazwbg

## Dream incubation

Preparing a question, theme or intention before sleep and recording what is recalled afterward.

Historical incubation includes particular ritual settings, while modern dream research also studies targeted suggestions around sleep. These contexts have different purposes and methods.

- Dream incubation / Incubación de sueños: Intentionally introducing a question or theme before sleep and observing the subsequent dream report.

- Ghibellini and Meier — The hypnagogic state: a brief update: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10078162/

## Hypnagogic imagery

Images, sounds or bodily impressions experienced during the transition from wakefulness into sleep.

Sleep research describes hypnagogia as a transition state with varied sensory experiences. A symbolic reading can accompany the record while the experience retains its sleep-boundary context.

- Hypnagogia / Hipnagogia: The transition from waking into sleep.
- Hypnagogic imagery / Imágenes hipnagógicas: Visual or other sensory experiences reported around sleep onset.

- Ghibellini and Meier — The hypnagogic state: a brief update: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10078162/

## Dream yoga & yoga nidra

Distinct contemplative practices concerning awareness, dreaming, rest and sleep, each with its own teaching context.

Tibetan Buddhist and Bön dream-yoga traditions and Indian yoga-nidra lineages should retain their own names and instructions. A shared interest in sleep does not make their aims or procedures interchangeable.

- Dream yoga / Yoga del sueño: Contemplative training involving dreaming and awareness in Tibetan Buddhist or Bön teaching contexts.
- Yoga nidra / Yoga nidra: A term used in Indian yogic traditions for practices of conscious rest or yogic sleep; specific methods depend on the lineage.

## Catoptromancy / mirror gazing

Contemplating or interpreting images seen in a reflective surface, including a dark mirror.

Mirrors and crystals occur in several scrying traditions. The instrument, lighting and viewing angle shape the visible image; the tradition supplies a separate interpretive vocabulary.

- Catoptromancy / Catoptromancia: A divinatory reading using a mirror or reflected images.
- Mirror gazing / Observación de espejos: Sustained visual attention to a reflective surface; the aim may be contemplative or symbolic.
- Dark mirror / Espejo oscuro: A dark reflective surface used as a gazing object in some scrying practices.

## Hydromancy

Symbolic or divinatory interpretation of water, its surface, reflections or movement.

Historical accounts describe more than one water-reading procedure. Reflections, ripples, vessel shape and light are physical observations that can be documented alongside a particular symbolic reading.

- Hydromancy / Hidromancia: A reading of water or phenomena observed in it, interpreted within a divinatory framework.

## Lecanomancy

A reading made with a basin, commonly involving water and sometimes floating substances such as oil.

The basin is the defining object in the historical term. Described variants use different liquids or additions, so the chosen source and materials matter more than a generic recipe.

- Lecanomancy / Lecanomancia: Divination with a basin and its contents, often water or oil on water.
- Lecanoscopy / Lecanoscopia: A related term for observing or reading a basin’s contents.

## Pyromancy

Pyromancy is a broad historical label. Candle gazing is a related focused practice; reading a flame’s symbolism and recording combustion conditions are different parts of the same field note.

- Pyromancy / Piromancia: Divinatory interpretation of fire or flame behavior.

## Lithomancy

Readings made with stones or gems through selection, arrangement, casting or reflected light.

The name covers differing historical and contemporary methods. Stone type, selection rules and assigned meanings should be specified rather than presented as a single universal system.

- Lithomancy / Litomancia: Divination involving stones or gems.

## Sand readings / psammomancy

Sand can be the material on which a named geomantic system is generated or simply the surface being interpreted. The field note states which procedure is intended.

## Inkblot & incidental-image readings

Symbolic responses to incidental marks, stains or ambiguous images, recorded as personal interpretations.

Chance-image reading can be a creative exercise. It is kept distinct from standardized psychological instruments such as the Rorschach, which have their own administration and scoring procedures.

- Inkblot reading / Lectura de manchas de tinta: Personal or symbolic interpretation of a shape made by ink.
- Incidental-image reading / Lectura de imágenes fortuitas: Interpreting an unplanned mark or naturally occurring configuration.
- Pareidolia / Pareidolia: Perceiving a familiar pattern or image in an ambiguous stimulus.

## Geomancy

A family of divinatory systems using generated figures, often formed from odd and even groups of marks.

The British Museum’s Islamic geomantic instrument documents figures known in the context of ʿilm al-raml. Other traditions use the word geomancy differently; the term alone does not identify a common procedure.

- Geomancy / Geomancia: A name for several traditions; here it primarily refers to divination through generated dot figures.
- ʿIlm al-raml / Ciencia de la arena: An Arabic name for the geomantic tradition literally associated with sand.
- Geomantic figure / Figura geomántica: A figure of single and double marks whose construction and meaning follow a stated geomantic system.

## Gematria & isopsephy

Letter-number interpretation within particular writing systems, including Hebrew and Greek traditions.

Gematria and Greek isopsephy depend on their languages’ letter values and textual conventions. Transliteration or a changed spelling can change a sum; numerical equality alone does not establish a shared historical meaning.

- Gematria / Gematría: Interpretation involving the numerical values of letters, particularly within Hebrew textual traditions.
- Isopsephy / Isopsefía: Greek letter-number calculation and comparison, including words or phrases with equal totals.

## Onomancy

Symbolic readings of names, their letters, sounds or assigned numerical correspondences.

Historical lexical definitions connect onomancy with the letters of names. Actual methods vary by language and source, so a modern correspondence table should not be presented as universal.

- Onomancy / Onomancia: Divinatory or symbolic interpretation of a name.

## Kledonomancy

Reading chance words, overheard phrases or incidental speech as symbolic prompts.

Pausanias describes an oracle of Hermes at Pharae involving the first words heard after a ritual inquiry. That account is one named historical setting rather than a rule for all incidental speech.

## Ifa, diloggun & named casting systems

Distinct named divinatory traditions whose signs, texts, materials and practitioners belong to particular communities.

Yoruba Ifá uses a corpus of odù and specialist interpretation. Lucumí diloggún uses cowrie-shell oracles in an Afro-Cuban religious context. Their related histories do not make their procedures or authority interchangeable.

- Ifá / Ifá: A Yoruba divinatory tradition using signs and an extensive odù literary corpus interpreted by trained practitioners.
- Diloggún / Diloggún: A cowrie-shell oracle in Lucumí orisha traditions, with its own protocols and interpretation.
- Odù / Odù: Named signs or divisions of the Ifá corpus; related terminology occurs in distinct traditions with its own context.

## Physiognomy & metoposcopy

Historical systems that attributed character, fate or other meanings to bodily appearance, especially facial and forehead features.

Early modern manuscripts preserve elaborate correspondences for faces and forehead lines. They are documented here as historical claims; appearance does not establish a person’s character, honesty, health or destiny.

- Physiognomy / Fisiognomía: Historical interpretation of outward features as supposed signs of character or fate.
- Metoposcopy / Metoposcopia: Historical readings of forehead lines and markings.

## Graphology

Interpretive systems that assign personal or symbolic meanings to features of handwriting.

Graphology differs from spontaneous writing and from forensic comparison of documents. A handwriting sample can be described materially without treating its appearance as a reliable measure of personality.

- Graphology / Grafología: Interpretation of handwriting features as signs of personal characteristics within a stated system.
- Handwriting description / Descripción de escritura manuscrita: Recording visible features such as spacing, slant, pressure marks and line form.

A movement journal extends the existing kinesthesia field note. It records felt motion and visible gesture while keeping any symbolic associations as the author’s interpretation.

- Movement journal / Diario de movimiento: A dated record of gestures, positions, movement sequences or bodily sensations.
- Kinesthetic journal / Diario kinestésico: A record centered on the felt experience of movement and position.

## Automatic music & improvisation

Spontaneous sound-making recorded before detailed editing or symbolic interpretation.

Automatist art sought to reduce conscious planning; musical improvisation has many separate artistic and cultural traditions. The recording preserves what was played, while the stated method explains its context.

- Automatic music / Música automática: A label for spontaneous music-making intended to reduce advance planning or conscious editing.
- Improvisation / Improvisación: Creating or developing a performance as it occurs, within or beyond stated musical rules.

## Tasseography variants

Related cup-reading practices interpreting patterns of tea leaves, coffee grounds or other specified sediment.

Preparation, cup orientation and symbol vocabularies differ between regional and family traditions. The source and preparation belong in the field note rather than a single universal list of meanings.

- Tasseography / Tasseografía: Interpretation of residue patterns in a cup or related vessel.
- Tea-leaf reading / Lectura de hojas de té: A tasseographic practice using leaves left in a cup.
- Coffee-ground reading / Lectura de posos de café: A tasseographic practice using coffee residue and its patterns.
