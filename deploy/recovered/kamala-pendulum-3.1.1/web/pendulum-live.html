READING THE INSTRUMENT

Image, motion,
observation.

Position & reference
The detected bell center, apparent radius and slowly adapting equilibrium reference remain in image pixels.
Offset & velocity
X / Y / Z movement is transmitted in millimeters and millimeters per second. Positive Z points away from the lens. The XY diagram is a planar projection.
Fit & calibration
Geometric fit is a matching score, not a probability or brightness reading. Samples at 15% or below are omitted from the traces. Z is inferred from apparent size; its accuracy requires a physical calibration test.
